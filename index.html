How it all started.

From curiosity to creation — the moments that shaped my path as a developer.

2019

Exploring Computers

I became curious about computers and started exploring websites, software, and the internet. As I learned how they worked, that curiosity naturally grew into an interest in programming and web development.

2022

Discovering Web Development

While exploring Chrome DevTools, I became fascinated by how websites were built. I started learning HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and realized I wanted to become a web developer.

2023

Learning the Fundamentals

I began creating simple websites with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript while studying through online resources. Every small project helped me build confidence and strengthen my understanding of web development.

2024

Building Projects

I shifted my focus from learning concepts to building real projects. Along the way, I learned Python, Git, and GitHub, and improved my problem-solving skills through practical experience and continuous development.

2026

Expanding Horizons

Focusing on smooth collaboration and thoughtful UI/UX details, ensuring even the smallest interactions delight the user. I am also diving into backend development to better understand the entire architecture and build more robust full-stack projects.
